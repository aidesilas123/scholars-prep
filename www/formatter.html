How to use:
1. Paste your raw question data (or entire CSV content) in the left box.
2. Click "Convert to Safe CSV Format".
3. Copy the result from the right box and save it as a .csv file.
4. Upload to Supabase. This tool automatically doubles backslashes (e.g., \lim → \\lim) and fixes JSON options.
